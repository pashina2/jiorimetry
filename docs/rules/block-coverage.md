# The list of blocks that have been reduced to rules (DC layer, 1.20.6, as of 2026-09-08)

> 日本語: [block-coverage.ja.md](block-coverage.ja.md)

"Reduced to rules" = a block whose behaviour was read out of the decompiled 1.20.6-yarn source, written up in our own words in a rule sheet (`docs/rules/`), solved by the Bench (`tools/llmgen/capcell.py` + `machine.py`) under those same rules, and confirmed to agree in the synthetic world and in the real world. No code is included. The layer of time (ticks / priming) is out of scope.

## Properties written in the rule sheets that are not yet used / not yet checked on the Bench
- The condition for preserving a value across a two-way wire branch (place it next to a strongly powered solid, do not make a wire→wire) has been used. The max merge of dust (several gates into one wire) has been used at r (through a solid).
- The "through a solid" container read (when the back is solid and i<15, the container beyond it is read) is in the rule sheet but unused in placement.
- The comparator read of an item frame is only recorded in the rule sheet.

## Out of scope (the layer of time; a separate rule sheet is needed)
observer, piston, hopper transport, note block, target, rail, sculk sensor, pressure plate, button (machine.py has partial implementations of hopper / lever / lamp / trapdoor / rail, but they are not written up as DC rules). The tick reservation of a comparator (priming) is out of scope too.
